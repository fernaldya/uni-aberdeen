Research question

State one clinically meaningful research question using the outcome allocated to your group. It should name your outcome measured over visits 0 to 6, one baseline characteristic or exposure of interest, and any other baseline characteristics that should be taken into account. It should make use of the repeated measurements and be worded as a question about association, not cause.

One pattern you may find helpful is “Among adults aged 60 to 75 in primary care, is [baseline characteristic] associated with [outcome] over six months of follow-up, taking account of [other baseline characteristics]?”

Research question:

Among adults aged 60 to 75 in primary care diagnosed with COPD, is the number of COPD exacerbations that require treatment during the previous 12 months associated with how far they are able walk within 6 minutes measured six times over 6 months, taking into account their usual level of physical activity?

It would be beneficial to understand whether there is a pattern in how far the participants are able to walk within 6 minutes during each follow up with how many COPD exacerbations they had that required treatment in the previous 12 months and whether their usual level of physical activity also affected the distance they are able to walk. If there is, then a further investigation into how much it affects and whether a baseline for each permutation exists by finding their means and standard deviation (or median and IQR). It is worth knowing the relationships between these variables as it could serve as a promoting point for having a better physical activity level despite having COPD.
